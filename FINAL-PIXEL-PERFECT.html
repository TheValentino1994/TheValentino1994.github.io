I'm a UX/UI designer with 5 years of experience turning complex product logic into interfaces that work — across fintech, crypto, SaaS, and mobile platforms.

My work starts with understanding the actual problem: flow analysis, friction mapping, and talking to the people who'll use it. Then wireframes, usability iterations, design systems, and handoff that developers don't have to guess at.

I work with product owners, engineers, and stakeholders to ship work that moves metrics and gets better with each release.

Design Philosophy

Every pixel should earn its place. I approach design as a craft where restraint is a feature, not a limitation.

The best interfaces disappear — they let people focus on what they came to do, not how to do it. I believe in systems over one-offs, clarity over decoration, and shipping over perfecting.

Design is a team sport, and the best outcomes come from designers who understand code, business, and the humans using the product.
