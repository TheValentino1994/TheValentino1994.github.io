## 📝 FULL TEXT FLOW:

### **SECTION 1: Challenge** ✅ (вже є, не міняємо)

**Title:** Turn fragmented admin work into connected workflows

**Text:**
The challenge was to create a web platform that could bring everyday business operations into one connected system - from scheduling and enrolments to payments, customer records, staff workflows, and communication.

The platform had to reduce manual work for admins, make daily tasks easier for coaches and staff, and give the business a clearer way to manage operations as it scaled.

---

### **SECTION 2: Approach** (переписати)

**Label:** Design Approach

**Title:** Understanding how coaches actually work

**Text:**
Started by mapping real workflow - morning check-ins, class coordination, end-of-day reconciliation. Wanted to see where time went and where friction happened.

Class Schedule emerged as the anchor point. Coaches opened it every morning, checked throughout the day, referenced it for every decision. Enrollments, payments, staff assignments - all connected back to it.

This revealed the strategy: don't just connect tools, organize around the main job. Make Class Schedule the starting point, show related info in context, eliminate navigation.

**Title:** Focus on what coaches actually need

**Intro text:**
Redesigned around how coaches work, not how software typically organizes features.

1. Quick access to daily workflow
2. Faster information scanning  
3. Works for any sport
4. Check schedules anywhere

---

### **SECTION 4: Process & Validation** (об'єднати останні дві)

**Label:** Process

**Title:** From prototype to launch

**Text:**
Redesigned core screens: Class Schedule, Students, Payments, Messages, Settings. Each screen followed same principle - show what coaches need for their current task, hide everything else.

Built interactive prototype and tested with client. They'd walk through daily workflows, point out friction. "This takes too many clicks" or "I need to see payment status here" - specific, actionable feedback.

Main pattern from testing: coaches valued speed over features. They wanted common tasks fast, not comprehensive options. This validated the approach - main workflow front and center, advanced features available but not competing for attention.

Delivered final designs and prototype. Client launched the platform. It's actively used today by coaches managing youth programs across different sports.
